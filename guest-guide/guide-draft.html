Welcome

Make yourself at home.

Ledgerbook is Michael’s home server. It shares a few things with family and friends he knows.

Which services do I have?

Michael gives you access to each service separately. If a section in this guide doesn’t work for you, ask him about access.

Start here

Your email gets you in.

Use these steps when a Ledgerbook web service shows a sign-in page. Each service may ask you to sign in separately the first time.

  1. Open the service’s web address.
  2. Enter the email address Michael registered for you.
  3. Check your email for a one-time code.
  4. Type the code into the sign-in page within a few minutes.

You’ll stay signed in for about a day on that device. After that, you’ll get a new code.

Keep your place.

Open the link in your regular browser, such as Safari, Chrome or Firefox. Browsers inside Messages, Discord or Facebook can lose the code page.

Keep the sign-in tab open while you check your email. Then return to that same tab to enter the code.

No code, or can’t get in?

Check spam, wait a minute, then try again. Codes expire.

Sign-in works only inside the US. If your VPN uses another country, choose a US location or turn the VPN off.

Open a service

  3. Sign in again if asked.

Return to your starting page
