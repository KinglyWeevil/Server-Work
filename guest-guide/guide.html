Welcome

Make yourself at home.

Ledgerbook is Michael’s home server. It shares a few things with family and friends he knows.

Which services do I have?

Your home page shows only the service tiles available to you. To see what else Ledgerbook offers, explore all available services. The directory describes each service, including those used only behind the scenes.

Start here

Your email gets you in.

When a Ledgerbook web service shows a sign-in page, follow these steps. Some services also have their own account, explained under Your accounts.

  1. Open the service’s web address.
  2. Enter the email address Michael registered for you.
  3. Check your email for a one-time code.
  4. Type the code into the sign-in page within a few minutes.

You stay signed in for about a day on that device. After that, you’ll get a new code.

Keep your place.

Open links in your regular browser: Chrome, Safari or Firefox. Browsers inside Messages, Discord or Facebook can lose the code page.

Keep the sign-in tab open while you fetch your code. Switch back to the same tab to enter it.

Sign-in works only inside the US. If your VPN is set to another country, choose a US location or turn it off.

No code? Check spam, wait a minute, then try again. Codes expire.

Each service may ask you to sign in separately the first time.

Your personal setup page

A place for your account details.

After signing in at ledgerbook.vip, open Your accounts. Only you and Michael can see your setup page. It lists your service usernames, starter passwords and setup links.

Open Your accounts ↗

https://ledgerbook.vip/setup

  1. Find the service you want on Your accounts.
  2. For Audiobookshelf or Requests, use its username and starter password to sign in.
  3. Change the starter password in that app’s account settings.
  4. Save your new password in a password manager, such as Proton Pass, Bitwarden or your phone’s built-in manager.

Some accounts work differently

Kavita: There is no starter password. Open the setup link emailed to you and choose your own password.

Plex: Accept Michael’s invitation email and use your own Plex account. There is no Ledgerbook password for Plex.

Private notes: Your accounts lists your username, password and the vault folder to open.

Keep your sign-in details private.

Never send passwords by email. Don’t write them in notes or texts.

Looking for a service you don’t have? Browse all available services. If a password won’t work, email concierge@ledgerbook.vip for a reset; don’t make a new account.

What it is

A movie and show player, like Netflix. Watch in the Plex app or on the Plex website.

  2. Choose a movie or show.
  3. Start playing it.

  2. Choose the title you want.

If something’s wrong

  2. Buffering? Choose a lower quality. Streaming quality depends on Michael’s home upload speed.
  3. Invitation missing? email concierge@ledgerbook.vip with the email used for Plex.

Still stuck? email concierge@ledgerbook.vip.

FAQ

A few quick answers.

Why do I keep getting codes?

Your sign-in lasts about a day on each device. Each service may ask you to sign in separately the first time.

Why does it say I’m blocked?

Ledgerbook sign-in works only inside the US. Check whether your VPN uses another country; choose a US location or turn it off.

Can my partner use my login?

Access is per person. For a separate account, email concierge@ledgerbook.vip.

How do I keep my account safe?

Keep your codes and sign-in details private. Use a password manager, and never send passwords by email.

Does this cost anything?

No. There is no charge to use these shared services.

I forgot a service password. What now?

For a reset, email concierge@ledgerbook.vip. Don’t create another account.
